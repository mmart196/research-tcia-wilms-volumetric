# Cover Letter — Cureus

Dear Editors,

We submit for your consideration our original article, "Protocol-Defined Volumetric
Response Profiles in Wilms Tumor: A Reproducible Analysis of Public Three-Dimensional
Segmentations From COG Trials AREN0532, AREN0533, and AREN0534."

Wilms tumor is the most common renal malignancy of childhood, yet its response to
neoadjuvant chemotherapy has never been quantified volumetrically at scale. Using
publicly available, expert-reviewed three-dimensional tumor segmentations from three
Children's Oncology Group trials hosted on The Cancer Imaging Archive, we measured
paired pre- and post-chemotherapy tumor volumes in 259 children and report the first
protocol-stratified volumetric response profiles for Wilms tumor. Median volume
reduction at the first post-chemotherapy assessment was 86% in the localized-disease
trial (AREN0532), 80% in the advanced-stage trial (AREN0533), and 72% in the
bilateral-disease trial (AREN0534), with 240 of 259 subjects showing a decrease.

Three features distinguish this work. First, framing: rather than pooling across
protocols, we report three protocol-defined response profiles and are explicit about
what descriptive volumetry can and cannot claim. Second, reproducibility: every
statistic in the manuscript regenerates from public data and publicly available code,
and the volume computation was validated against the annotation tool's own
measurements (Spearman ρ = 0.998 across 1,141 lesions). Third, honesty about scope:
the analysis is deliberately contour-based and we state plainly what the public data
cannot show.

The study uses only public, de-identified data and required no institutional review
board approval. The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere, and all authors
have approved this submission. We declare no conflicts of interest and no external
funding. We believe Cureus's readership — clinicians engaged in open, reproducible
science — is the natural audience for this work.

Thank you for your consideration.
